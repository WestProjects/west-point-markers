Historical Society of West Point

The Historical Society preserves and shares West Point's story — from its shipping and oyster-house years on the Pamunkey and Mattaponi to the homes, schools, and hotels that shaped downtown. The Society maintains the town's brass historical markers and runs a small museum inside the current Society building, once the Mumford Bank and Grove Hotel.
